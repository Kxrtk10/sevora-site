Questions owners ask

Does it sound like a robot?

The real Voice Desk uses natural, Indian-accented voices built for phone calls. This demo uses your browser’s built-in voice, so it sounds flatter than the real thing.

Will callers know it’s an assistant?

Yes. It introduces itself as your business’s AI assistant at the start of every call, and anyone can ask for a person at any time.

What happens in an emergency?

It asks the safety questions you give it. Anything urgent goes straight through to the on-call person by call and SMS, and the caller is told to go to a hospital if there are danger signs.

Are calls recorded?

Only if you want them to be, and callers are told at the start. Summaries are kept in your own accounts.

Can it handle several calls at once?

Yes. Unlike a receptionist, it can answer many calls at the same time, so nobody hears a busy tone.
