Questions owners ask

Will it make decisions without me?

Only the ones you allow. Each area has its own setting: “just do it”, “tell me after”, or “ask me”. Anything involving prices, hiring, contracts or large spends waits for your tap unless you change that yourself.

Where do the numbers come from?

Your own systems first: POS or billing, accounts, bookings, reviews, delivery-app statements. Public data like nearby prices and seasonal trends is added and labelled. If the data isn’t good enough to decide, the card says so and the confidence drops.

What if a recommendation is wrong?

Every card shows a worst case before you approve. After you approve, the outcome is tracked against the prediction. If a type of decision keeps missing, the agents lower their confidence and tell you why.

Is my data safe?

Your data stays in your own accounts and a private workspace for your business. It isn’t used to train public models, and you can disconnect any source at any time.

Do I need new software?

No. We connect what you already use: Google Sheets, your POS or Tally export, WhatsApp, payment and delivery-app statements. The decisions come to you on WhatsApp and on this kind of page.

Is this only for cafés?

No. The café is the demo. The same network works for clinics, gyms, shops, real estate, agencies and small manufacturers: anywhere decisions depend on numbers you already have.
