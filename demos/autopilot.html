Questions owners ask

Will it spend my money without asking?

Only inside limits you set. For example: supplier orders up to ₹5,000, refunds up to ₹500, overtime up to ₹1,000. Anything above a limit waits for your tap, with the numbers in front of you.

Do I need new software?

Usually not. We connect what you already use: your POS or billing software, WhatsApp Business, Razorpay or your UPI provider, Tally or Zoho Books, Google reviews, Swiggy and Zomato, Google Calendar or your clinic software.

What if an agent gets something wrong?

Every action is logged with the reason, so mistakes are easy to spot and undo. During the pilot we review the log with you every week and tighten the rules until you trust it.

Will it replace my staff?

It takes the repetitive work off them: messages, reminders, stock counts, reconciliations, follow-ups. Your team spends that time on customers and patients instead.

Is this real, or just a demo?

The day here is a replay with demo numbers so you can see the whole picture in 90 seconds. Every piece in it, from WhatsApp orders and calls to stock alerts, reconciliation and review replies, is something we build today with real tools.

Is my data safe?

Your data stays in your own accounts and a private workspace for your business. It isn’t used to train public models, and you can disconnect anything at any time.
