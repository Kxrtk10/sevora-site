The idea

Areté is the old Greek word for excellence — the quality of living up to everything you are capable of. Not a number on a scale. A standard you keep, one session at a time.

1:6Coach-to-member ratio on every coached session.
12Weeks in every programme block, with a scan at each end.

The craft

Strength is a skill.We teach it.

Every member learns the lifts properly before they load them. Chalk, a coach and a plan — the rest is repetition.
