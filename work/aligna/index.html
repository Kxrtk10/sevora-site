Questions

Before your first visit.

Does an implant hurt?

The procedure is done under local anaesthesia and most patients compare it to a filling. Expect two or three days of mild soreness, managed with regular painkillers.

Can I get it done in one visit?

Root canals and fillings, usually yes. Implants need the bone to heal around the screw, so the crown goes on 3 to 6 months later. You leave with a temporary tooth in the meantime.

Do you accept insurance?

We provide itemised bills for reimbursement with all major insurers. Most dental insurance in India covers treatment after an accident; cosmetic work usually isn't covered.

What if I have a dental emergency at night?

Call the clinic number. The AI receptionist answers, collects your details and alerts the on-call dentist, who calls you back.

How do aligners compare to braces?

Aligners are near-invisible and removable, and suit most mild to moderate cases. Braces handle complex movements better. Dr. Kumar decides after a 3D scan.
