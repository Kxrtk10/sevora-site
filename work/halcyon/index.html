Questions

Before you reserve.

Does it work with iPhone and Android?

Yes. H1 supports AAC for iPhone and LDAC for Android, and connects to two devices at once. The Halcyon app runs on both.

Can I use it wired?

Yes, over USB-C with full 24-bit/96 kHz audio, and noise cancelling still works. A 3.5 mm cable is in the box for flights.

How heavy is it?

268 grams. The clamp force is set low for glasses wearers, and the headband pad spreads the weight across the top of your head.

What happens if a part breaks after warranty?

Order the part from the list above. Hand-replaceable parts ship in two days; driver and board repairs go through our Hyderabad service centre.

When will my card be charged?

Never through this form. You get a payment link by email about a week before your unit ships, and you can cancel before paying.
